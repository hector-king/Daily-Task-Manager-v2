TASK MANAGER - PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how the Task Manager app handles information when you use
it. It describes the app's current behavior.

INFORMATION YOU ENTER

Task Manager lets you create and manage tasks. The information you enter can
include task names, notes, due dates and times, priority levels, and completion
status. The app creates an identifier for each task so it can manage your tasks
and their reminders.

WHERE YOUR INFORMATION IS STORED

Your tasks are stored on your device in the app's local storage. The app does
not require an account, and the app's current functionality does not send your
tasks to the app developer or to an app-operated server. The app does not
provide cloud sync.

Task data remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Your
device's operating system may back up app data if device backup is enabled.
Those backups are governed by your device provider's settings and policies.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you allow notifications, the app can schedule a local reminder for an
upcoming task, normally 10 minutes before its due time. For a task due within
10 minutes, the app may show a reminder immediately. The task name may appear
in the notification. Reminders are managed on your device; the app does not
send task details to a notification service operated by the app developer.

The app uses your device's local time zone to schedule reminders. Android may
also restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts. You can deny or
revoke notification permission in your device settings. If notifications are
disabled, reminders may not appear.

SHARING AND TRACKING

The app's current functionality does not include advertising, analytics, or
account tracking, and it does not sell or share your task information with the
app developer or other organizations. Notifications are delivered through
Android's on-device notification system. Device backups, if enabled, are
controlled by your device provider as described above.

PERMISSIONS

On Android, the app may request permission to show notifications. It also uses
Android's device-restart event so scheduled reminders can be restored after a
restart. It does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or
location for its task-management features.

YOUR CHOICES

You can edit or delete tasks within the app. Deleting a task also cancels its
pending reminder. You can manage notification permission through Android
settings. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to any device
backup you have enabled.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Task Manager does not require users to provide personal information to the
developer. If you choose to put personal information in a task name or note,
that information is stored locally as described above.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data practices change.
The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised policy is issued.
